History

Exciting Expeditions was founded in 1995 by a dedicated group of professional outdoor adventure enthusiasts who wanted to share their profound love for nature and wild rivers with the world. Starting with just a handful of rugged rubber rafts and an unyielding passion for exploration, the company has steadily grown into a nationally recognized leader in providing safe, premier whitewater rafting experiences for families, corporate teams, and extreme sports enthusiasts alike.
